01

JEE PREPARATION TIPS

Build a routine you can actually keep

Set a realistic daily target, protect focused study blocks, and leave time for sleep and breaks. Increase your workload gradually rather than starting with an unsustainable schedule.

02

REVISION STRATEGY

Recall first, reread second

Close your notes and try to explain key ideas, derivations, and formulas from memory. Use the gaps you discover to guide a focused review, then revisit the chapter again later.

03

MOCK TESTS

Turn every test into a learning loop

Review incorrect answers, guessed answers, and problems that took too long. Record what you learned, and practise those specific concepts before your next attempt.

IMPORTANT ANNOUNCEMENTS

Stay with official updates

Exam dates, application windows, eligibility, and syllabus can change. Check the official NTA and JEE Advanced websites before relying on announcements shared elsewhere.
